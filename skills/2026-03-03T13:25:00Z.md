## Core Idea

Instead of asking vague questions, follow a structured protocol to resolve uncertainty independently first. This cultivates a “researcher’s instinct” and ensures that questions you do ask are precise and meaningful.

## Protocol

Ask yourself - Should it be a Google search, asking an AI for explanation or a research question that needs the perspective of the other person?

1. **The Look-up**  
   Search thoroughly: Google, official sources, and alternative AI tools. Check multiple perspectives or explanations.  

2. **The Diagnosis**  
   Identify exactly what is confusing. Pinpoint the line, term, or clause in a document that contradicts your understanding or raises doubt.  

3. **The Approach**  
   Frame your question based on evidence and observation:  
   - Instead of: "What does this mean?"  
   - Ask: "I found Document X and Document Y; they seem to contradict on this point—is my interpretation of the friction correct?"  

## Why This Matters

- Promotes independent thinking and problem-solving.  
- Makes your questions actionable and precise.  
- Builds credibility and demonstrates rigorous reasoning in research.
