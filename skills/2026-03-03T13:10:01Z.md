## Core Idea

Maintain a methodology section that explains how you arrived at each insight, including tools, searches, and reasoning steps. This makes your work reproducible and demonstrates your critical thinking.

## Why This Matters

- Helps you refine your approach by observing patterns in problem-solving.  
- Enables peers or supervisors to understand and verify your workflow.  
- Supports teaching, collaboration, and scaling research.  

## Example From Context

The lab lead advised the student to write down how they found the first clash between FSSAI and ITC-HS codes. Documenting steps—from identifying categories, using AI for searches, and cross-referencing regulations—creates a “mental model” for the methodology section.

## How To Apply

1. After completing a logic block, note every step: sources consulted, search terms, filters applied.  
2. Mention tools or AI used for document discovery (e.g., Gemini, Perplexity).  
3. Record challenges encountered and dead ends.  
4. Structure this as a “Methodology” section in the research document.  
5. Review periodically to refine and improve your search strategy.
