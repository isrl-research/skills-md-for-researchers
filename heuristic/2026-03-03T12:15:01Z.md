## Core Idea

Only take on or assign new work when you have sufficient mental bandwidth to focus and perform effectively.

## Why This Matters

Cognitive performance decreases when you are fatigued or overloaded. Taking on new tasks during high-exertion periods reduces quality, increases errors, and adds unnecessary stress. Timing tasks according to mental bandwidth improves execution and sustainability.

## Example From Context

The sender advises you to rest after startup pitches and only pick up new tasks once you have regained mental bandwidth, ensuring your next task is handled effectively.

## How To Apply

- Acknowledge when you are under intense workload before taking on additional tasks.
- Wait until you have recovered focus and energy before starting new assignments.
- Sequence high-cognitive tasks after rest or low-demand periods.
- Signal explicitly when you are ready to take on more work.
- Prioritize quality and sustainability over immediate task turnover.
