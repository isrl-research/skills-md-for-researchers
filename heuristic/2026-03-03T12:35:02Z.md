## Core Idea

Assess whether your writing is neutral by imagining how stakeholders would react if reading it as a routine report rather than as an evaluation of their decisions.

## Why This Matters

Neutrality is context-sensitive. Wording that seems factual to the writer may appear flattering, offensive, or biased to others. A structured perspective check reduces these risks and ensures your documentation is received as intended.

## Example From Context

The email suggests imagining three perspectives: FSSAI regulators, industry participants, and neutral reviewers. If the text reads like a normal workday report to each, it is neutral. If it offends or flatters, the language is biased.

## How To Apply

- Read your text as if you are each stakeholder (regulator, industry, neutral observer).
- Ask: "Does this read like a standard work report?" If yes, keep it; if not, revise.
- Avoid adjectives that imply judgment or praise.
- Use objective descriptions of changes, laws, or events.
- Repeat this test iteratively for each section of your document.
