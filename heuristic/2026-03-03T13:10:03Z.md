## Core Idea

Leverage AI to locate documents, navigate long PDFs, or identify sections of interest, but ensure that the reasoning, interpretation, and conclusions are your own.

## Why This Matters

Over-reliance on AI can lead to errors, misinterpretation, or unverified claims. Using AI responsibly increases efficiency while maintaining research integrity.

## Example From Context

The student used AI to understand basics and to locate documents. The lab lead confirmed that AI for resource discovery is fine, but emphasized that analytical thinking and logical conclusions must originate from the researcher.

## How To Apply

1. Use AI for document discovery, keyword searching, or summarizing large text blocks.  
2. Independently verify AI-suggested results against primary sources.  
3. Record your reasoning and explain how the AI output informed your analysis.  
4. Avoid letting AI generate interpretations or conclusions for your research.
